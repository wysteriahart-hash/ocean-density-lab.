3. Investigate

  1. Keep mass at 12 g. Decrease volume from 12 to 6 mL. What happens to particle spacing and density?
  2. Reset ocean conditions. Lower temperature from 15 °C to 5 °C. Record the change in density.
  3. Reset again. Increase salinity from 35 to 40 PSU. Record the change in density.
  4. Predict which sinks beneath the other: cold salty seawater or warm freshwater. Explain using density.

Key idea: More mass in the same volume means greater density. Cooling seawater generally increases its density, and adding dissolved salt increases mass per unit volume. Real ocean circulation also depends on wind, geography, and other forces.
